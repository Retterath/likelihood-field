Check an endpoint to project it onto the map. Its x and y decide its distance to the nearest occupied cell, which sets its score. Click a cell to make it occupied or free.

How the field is built

Only occupied cells carry a Gaussian. An endpoint is scored by its distance to the nearest occupied cell (the nearest one wins; Gaussians aren’t added), so endpoints in open space fall to the random-noise floor.

Sensor parameters

zrand = 1 − zhit, zmax = 10 m. Each endpoint scores zhit·prob(dist, σhit) + zrand/zmax.
